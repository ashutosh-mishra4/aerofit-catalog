A day on the band, from alarm to lights out

You wear it and forget it. Aero Fit only speaks up when there is something worth acting on.

  1. 6:40

    Wake to a plan

    Your Recovery score is waiting, with a strain target sized to how well you slept.

  2. 12:15

    Steady through the day

    Strain builds quietly in the background. A nudge arrives if you have been still too long.

  3. 18:30

    Train with feedback

    Live heart rate zones and GPS on your wrist, then a breakdown of what the session cost you.

  4. 22:50

    Wind down on time

    A bedtime suggestion based on tomorrow’s plan, so your sleep matches the day ahead.
